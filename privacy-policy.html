中文

本政策适用于 Postcard Routes Studio 提供的 Android 游戏《符文封印》（Rune Seal）。

1. 我们处理哪些数据

设备本地数据

游戏使用 Unity PlayerPrefs 在设备本地保存关卡进度、完成记录、音乐与音效、触感、简化动态效果、增强对比、新手引导和广告隐私界面的本地状态。我们不运营玩家账号、云存档或玩家数据服务器，这些本地进度不会发送给我们。

Google 广告服务数据

游戏接入 Google Mobile Ads（AdMob）SDK 和 Google User Messaging Platform（UMP）。Google 可能自动收集或共享 IP 地址（并推断大致位置）、应用启动和广告互动、崩溃及性能诊断信息，以及 Android 广告 ID、应用集 ID 等设备或账号标识符，用于广告投放与个性化、频次控制、衡量、分析、防欺诈、安全和合规。实际处理受地区、同意选择、设备设置和 Google 政策影响。

详细说明见 Google Mobile Ads SDK 数据披露和 Google 隐私权政策。我们不会通过广告获得玩家的真实姓名、通讯录、短信、聊天内容或精确位置，也不会把本地关卡进度提供给广告平台。

2. 广告形式与选择

  • 激励广告只在玩家主动选择后请求，Google 确认奖励后才发放礼包或续关奖励。
  • 插屏广告只在部分关卡完成后的自然间隙请求。
  • 自适应横幅只出现在首页和关卡选择等非对局页面。

适用地区会显示 UMP 隐私或同意界面；需要时可从“设置 → 广告隐私选项”重新打开。玩家也可通过 Google 我的广告中心管理广告选择。拒绝个性化广告不一定移除全部广告；Google 仍可能提供非个性化或受限广告并处理安全、防欺诈等必要数据。

3. 第三方、权限与安全

当前联网第三方服务为 Google Mobile Ads 和 UMP。数据会为上述目的传送给 Google 及其广告技术合作方。我们不出售个人数据，也不向数据经纪商提供玩家数据。当前版本未接入账号、支付、云存档、Firebase Analytics、Unity Analytics、Unity Ads、Sentry 或开发者自建分析服务。

INTERNET 用于广告和同意界面，VIBRATE 仅用于玩家开启的触感；广告 SDK 可能声明广告 ID 权限。应用不请求相机、麦克风、通讯录、短信、通话记录、通知或精确位置权限。Google 表示 Mobile Ads SDK 使用 TLS 加密传输数据。

4. 保存、删除与控制

本地数据会保留到玩家清除应用数据或卸载游戏为止，可通过 Android“应用信息 → 存储 → 清除数据”删除。我们没有服务器副本，无法远程删除、恢复或导出本地数据。Google 依其政策和玩家设置管理广告数据；玩家可重置或删除 Android 广告 ID，并使用 Google 账号隐私工具或“我的广告中心”。

5. 儿童隐私与国际处理

本游戏面向一般成年休闲益智玩家，并非专门面向儿童。我们不会故意收集 13 岁以下儿童（或当地法律规定的更高最低年龄）的个人数据；在完成相应合规评估前，不会选择 13 岁以下 Google Play 目标受众。Google 可能在玩家所在国家或地区之外处理广告数据，并采用其隐私政策说明的保障措施。

6. 联系与更新

如对本政策、广告隐私选择或游戏的数据处理有疑问，请联系 Postcard Routes Studio：weiwei199344@gmail.com。数据实践、SDK、权限或法律要求变化时，我们会更新本政策和日期，并在适当位置通知重大变化。

English

This policy applies to the Android game Rune Seal, provided by Postcard Routes Studio.

1. Data we process

On-device data

Unity PlayerPrefs stores level progress, completion records, audio and haptic preferences, reduced motion, high contrast, onboarding, and local advertising-privacy interface state. We operate no player accounts, cloud saves, or player-data server, and local gameplay progress is not sent to us.

Google advertising data

The game integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). Google may automatically collect or share IP address (and estimate general location), app launches and ad interactions, crash and performance diagnostics, Android advertising ID, app set ID, and other device or account identifiers. Purposes may include ad delivery and personalization, frequency capping, measurement, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and compliance. Processing depends on region, consent, device settings, and Google policies.

See the Google Mobile Ads SDK disclosure and Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive a player's real name, contacts, messages, chat content, or precise location through the ads, and do not provide local level progress to the ad platform.

2. Advertising and choice

  • Rewarded ads are requested only after a player chooses to watch one; gifts are granted only after Google confirms the reward.
  • Interstitials are requested only at natural breaks after selected completed levels.
  • Adaptive banners appear only on non-gameplay screens such as home and level select.

Where required, UMP presents a privacy or consent message and can be reopened from Settings → Ad privacy options. Players can also use My Ad Center. Refusing personalized ads does not necessarily remove all ads; Google may still provide non-personalized or limited ads and process data needed for security and fraud prevention.

3. Third parties, permissions, and security

The current networked third parties are Google Mobile Ads and UMP. Information is transmitted to Google and its ad-technology partners for the stated purposes. We do not sell personal data or provide it to data brokers. The current release has no player accounts, payments, cloud saves, Firebase Analytics, Unity Analytics, Unity Ads, Sentry, or developer-operated analytics.

INTERNET loads ads and consent interfaces; VIBRATE supports player-enabled haptics; the ad SDK may declare advertising-ID permissions. The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, call-log, notification, or precise-location permissions. Google states that the Mobile Ads SDK encrypts data in transit using TLS.

4. Retention, deletion, and controls

Local data remains until the player clears app storage or uninstalls the game and can be removed through Android App info → Storage → Clear data. We have no server copy to delete, restore, or export. Google controls advertising-data retention under its policies and player settings; players can reset or delete the Android advertising ID and use Google privacy tools or My Ad Center.

5. Children and international processing

Rune Seal is intended for a general adult casual-puzzle audience and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 or a higher local minimum age and will not select a Google Play audience below 13 without a separate compliance review. Google may process ad information outside the player's country or region using safeguards described in its privacy policy.

6. Contact and changes

For questions about this policy, advertising privacy choices, or the game's data practices, contact Postcard Routes Studio at weiwei199344@gmail.com. We will update this policy and date when data practices, SDKs, permissions, or legal requirements change and will provide appropriate notice of material changes.
